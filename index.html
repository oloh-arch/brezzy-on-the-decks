About The DJ

Master of the Decks & Kenyan Sound

BREZZY ON THE DECKS (known as DJ Messi / The Record Breaker) is a dynamic Kenyan DJ mix creator pushing the boundaries of East African urban music.

Specializing in high-energy blends of Kenyan Trap & Drill, Gengetone, Afrobeats, Amapiano, and R&B, Brezzy brings a distinct hype and seamless mixing style to listeners across Kenya and internationally.

Whether curating high-energy club sessions, viral YouTube mixsets, or hosting special tribute projects like the Best of Virusi Mbaya Vol. 2, Brezzy delivers an unmatched musical experience.
